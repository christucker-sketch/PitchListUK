# PitchList UK SEO Strategy

Reworked 2026-05-10 after live demand checks showed traders do not mainly search for “event pitch finder”.

## Core SEO decision

PitchList should rank around the language traders already use:

- **festival trader applications**
- **stall holders wanted** / **stallholders wanted**
- **market stall holders wanted**
- **food traders wanted**
- **festival vendors wanted**
- **events looking for vendors / stallholders**
- **where to sell street food UK**
- **how to find events to sell at**

Avoid leading with internal/product language such as:

- event pitch finder
- pitch opportunities
- pitch list

Those can remain secondary/supporting phrases, but not the main acquisition strategy.

## Positioning

Primary proposition:

> Find UK festival trader applications, stallholder opportunities and food-trading events without spending hours searching organiser websites, council pages and social posts.

Plain-English title formula:

> UK Festival Trader Applications & Stallholder Opportunities

## Search intent groups

### 1. High-intent trader/application searches

Target users actively looking for places to apply.

Primary terms:

- festival trader applications
- festival trader applications 2026
- festival trader application UK
- food festival trader applications
- festival vendors wanted
- food traders wanted

Best page:

- `/festival-trader-applications.html`

CTA:

- “Request 5 sample rows for your area”

### 2. Stallholder/event opportunity searches

Target crafts, artisan food, drinks, gift, market and seasonal traders.

Primary terms:

- stall holders wanted
- stallholders wanted
- stall holders wanted near me
- market stall holders wanted
- stall holders needed
- christmas stall holders wanted

Best page:

- `/stall-holders-wanted.html`

CTA:

- “Tell us your area and what you sell”

### 3. Street food / mobile catering searches

Target food trucks, vans, gazebos and mobile caterers.

Primary terms:

- food traders wanted UK
- street food trader opportunities
- where to sell street food UK
- food truck events UK
- mobile caterers wanted
- pop up food stall opportunities

Best page:

- `/food-traders-wanted.html`

CTA:

- “Get area-matched food trading leads”

### 4. Informational/problem searches

Target people who know the pain but not the solution.

Primary terms:

- how to find events to sell at
- how to find vendor events UK
- how to sell at a vendor event
- where to find market stall opportunities

Best format:

- Future blog/help pages once the MVP is stable.

Recommended first article:

- `/how-to-find-events-to-sell-at.html`

The homepage should not try to rank for everything. It should be the conversion hub and brand landing page.

Homepage H1:

- mobile caterers

- `/christmas-stall-holders-wanted.html`
- `/market-stall-opportunities.html`
- `/street-food-trader-opportunities.html`
- region pages only when there is enough data to avoid thin SEO spam, e.g. `/kent-stallholder-opportunities.html`.

## On-page rules

Every SEO page should include:

- One exact-match primary phrase in title/H1.
- Related phrases in H2s, body copy and internal links.
- Clear buyer CTA above the fold.
- Trust language: source-linked rows, last checked, no fake leads, no acceptance guarantee.
- Area-first framing: users care where they can trade.
- “Free sample before payment” to reduce risk.

Avoid:

- Thin doorway pages per county before we have coverage.
- Overpromising guaranteed pitches, sales, footfall or acceptance.
- Publishing full live lead rows publicly, because the application route is the product value.

## Measurement plan

When Search Console is connected, review weekly:

- Queries containing: trader application, stall holder, stallholder, food traders wanted, vendors wanted, events to sell at.
- Pages getting impressions but low CTR — rewrite title/meta first.
- Pages with clicks but no sample requests — tighten CTA and sample proof.
- Geographic query patterns — only then create region pages.
