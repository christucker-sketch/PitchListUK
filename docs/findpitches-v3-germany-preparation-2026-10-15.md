## Requirements before a German canary

1. **Country and identity:** bind the actual event venue to defensible German geography. German language, `.de`, `+49`, a five-digit postcode, a query city or the organiser's registered office alone are insufficient. German-language Austrian/Swiss events stay AT/CH. Use `DE-BY` etc.; bare `DE` must not collide with US Delaware. Do not silently change an existing identity or edition.
2. **Dates and lifecycle:** parse German month names and numeric dates only inside identified event/deadline fields. Require explicit source-backed year/edition. Keep PDF issue dates, “Stand”, copyright years, page clocks and event dates separate. Respect cancellation, postponement, application deadline, daily closures and recurring schedules. Preserve exact source closing times/time zones; a date-only deadline must not acquire an invented cutoff or unsupported open-state claim.
3. **Application roles:** distinguish a current prospective-exhibitor intake from existing-vendor login, visitor ticketing, sponsor forms, generic contact, employment applications and unrelated procurement. `Bewerbung`, `Aussteller`, `Beschicker`, `Standanmeldung`, `Zulassung` and `Standplatz` require page context. Under D-018 an explicitly organiser-controlled standing trader enquiry may qualify, labelled honestly as enquiry/registration, not guaranteed open availability.
4. **Source-specific proof:** use the exact official event → route/PDF links, allowlisted family paths and custody hashes. Each document retains its own observed time; combined proof cannot become fresher than its oldest dependency. Exclude navigation/footer links, contradictory editions and soft-404s. PDF text extraction must be bounded; scans, broken text layers or contradictory documents stay held. Never use a document filename alone as proof.
5. **Commercial fit:** distinguish public-facing trader opportunities from private sellers-only flea markets, wholesale/B2B-only trade shows and visitor exhibitions. Record relevant trader restrictions, product criteria, fees and enquiry semantics without inventing them. A weekly series or multi-day fair must not inflate into duplicate entities per date/document.
6. **Runtime compatibility:** implement and test DE country/intake/staging/customer contracts together before any DE export; this is still future work. Preserve global Pro/free redaction, provenance, receipt replay and stale-recheck guards. Exact venue pins require venue-specific coordinates or a suitable defensible resolver; no city/postcode centres or US Census fallback for Germany.
