# FindPitches V3 — global usable-route policy

Authority: **D-018 / Chris, 2026-10-13**.

This policy applies to the **entire FindPitches global catalogue**, not only the UK.

## Core rule

A current/future opportunity may be customer-ready when:

1. the event/opportunity identity is sufficiently proven;
2. its relevant current/future date or ongoing/current-cycle status is sufficiently proven;
3. its location/country and trader/vendor relevance are sufficiently proven; and
4. there is a **current organiser-controlled route through which a prospective trader/vendor can reasonably take the next commercial step now**.

The route **does not need to be edition-specific** if the organiser clearly uses a standing or organiser-wide intake process for the relevant event/programme.

This change deliberately broadens route semantics. It does **not** loosen event identity, date/edition, geography, organiser ownership, lifecycle or anti-fabrication requirements.

## Valid usable routes

The following count as usable when current, working, organiser-controlled and relevant to prospective traders/vendors:

- an event-specific vendor/trader/stallholder application form;
- a generic organiser-wide vendor/trader application or registration form;
- a standing registration used for multiple markets/events/dates;
- an organiser page explicitly inviting people to apply to trade, become a vendor, exhibit or book a stall;
- a trader/vendor/stallholder enquiry form where the page explicitly states it is for those enquiries;
- a trader/vendor/stallholder email address explicitly published for applications or enquiries;
- an organiser-directed vendor portal or account-registration route for prospective vendors;
- an event or organiser page that explicitly instructs prospective traders/vendors to contact or enquire through a named current route.

A route may therefore support an **actionable enquiry/registration outcome** even when it is not a one-click exact-event application.

## Not usable

Do not count the following by themselves:

- generic contact forms with no trader/vendor/stallholder context;
- generic office emails with no evidence they handle vendor applications/enquiries;
- social-media DMs or social pages as the only route;
- newsletter forms;
- customer ticket-purchase links;
- vendor login pages intended only for already-approved traders when there is no prospective-vendor signup route;
- aggregator/directory pages that merely mention vendors wanted but provide no organiser-controlled route;
- dead, expired or clearly historical application forms;
- an old-edition route with no evidence it remains current for the organiser/programme;
- guessed contact details or inferred routes.

## Standing-route semantics

A standing organiser route is sufficient when all of the following are true:

- the organiser controls the route;
- the route is currently live;
- the route is explicitly for prospective traders/vendors/stallholders/exhibitors;
- the event/opportunity is proved to belong to that organiser or programme;
- nothing on the current source says that event/programme is closed to applications or uses a different route.

Do not require a separate exact-event form merely because the organiser operates one central vendor-intake process.

## Customer status / wording

Where the route is an exact active application, existing OPEN/APPLY semantics may be used when otherwise justified.

Where the route is a standing registration, vendor intake or trader-specific enquiry rather than an exact event application, surface it truthfully as actionable registration/enquiry/contact semantics rather than falsely claiming that an exact application is open.

The customer should still be able to take the next legitimate commercial step from the listing.

## Global re-evaluation requirement

Codex should re-evaluate withheld/non-READY records across **all current markets** against this policy, prioritising populations currently blocked only by narrow route semantics.

Measure and report:

- records promoted by route type;
- READY/actionable gains by country;
- standing-registration gains;
- trader-specific enquiry/email gains;
- false-positive/rollback findings;
- any remaining major route blocker classes.

Do not bulk-promote records lacking proven identity/date/location/trader relevance. Route-policy expansion is not permission to weaken the other proof layers.

## Relationship to earlier decisions

This policy generalises and expands D-012 (enquiry-only routes) globally. D-012 remains valid as a specific example, but D-018 is now the controlling route-semantics policy for the global catalogue.

UK growth D-017 continues, but D-018 is not UK-specific and should be implemented consistently across US, UK, IE, CA, AU, NZ and future markets.
