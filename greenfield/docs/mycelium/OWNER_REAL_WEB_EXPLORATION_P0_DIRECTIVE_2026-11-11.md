## Non-negotiable product model
**Mycelium is a persistent, multidirectional, open-ended web explorer.** It grows a revisitable frontier by following actual breadcrumbs it encounters: hyperlinks, PDF/document links, event/venue/organiser relationships, references, associations, school/PTA/parish pages, sports organisations, public directories, cross-language and cross-border relationships. A clue is valuable even when the current page is not a saleable pitch; it may open an entire network of sources. New paths emerge from observations, **not from a developer-defined list of lanes, countries, event categories or a keyword generator masquerading as curiosity**.

Separate:
1. **Exploration:** raw evidence, provenance, links and context, expanding frontier, uncertainty, dead ends and revisit triggers.
2. **Understanding:** connected source/entity/venue/organiser/event claims, contradictions, novelty, hypotheses and cross-branch links.
3. **Commercial verification:** customer-usable pitch, current date/status, credible organiser and public application/enquiry route. Never promote mere event mentions or speculation to confirmed opportunities.
4. **FindPitches delivery:** independently scoped customer-facing application across countries/languages. Mycelium's core must not embed FindPitches eligibility as its attention or storage schema.

No claim of AGI/emergent intelligence is an acceptance metric.
