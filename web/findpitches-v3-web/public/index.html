    Good to know

    How do I find events to sell at?

    Search FindPitches with your postcode or ZIP Code and what you sell. You'll see markets, festivals, shows and sporting events near you that are looking for traders, with dates, location and a direct route to apply.

    Do you guarantee I'll get a pitch?

    No. Organisers choose their traders. What we promise is that every listing is real, comes from the organiser, and gets you to the right application first time.

    Where do the listings come from?

    Official organisers, councils, venues, stadiums and event operators. We don't copy aggregator lists, and every listing shows when we last checked it.

    Do you list sporting events and stadium concessions?

    Yes. Marathons and race expos, rodeos, racecourses, motorsport and tournaments are all in. Sports is a fast-growing part of FindPitches, especially in the US.

    What does it cost?

    Searching and saving are free. Pro adds the organiser’s application page for every listing, plus alerts.

    Which countries do you cover?

    FindPitches is built for eight markets, each with its own local finder.

    I run events. How do I get listed?

    Listing your call for traders is free. Send us the link and we'll check it against your own page before it goes live.
