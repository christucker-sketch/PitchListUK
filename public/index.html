The problem

Finding pitch applications takes longer than it should.

Pitch opportunities are spread across festival websites, council market pages, organiser forms, showground trader packs, farmers market directories, shopping-centre leasing pages, university catering routes, brewery taproom calendars, sports-event concession pages, fireworks nights, car shows and social posts. The useful information is rarely in one place, and deadlines are often buried.
