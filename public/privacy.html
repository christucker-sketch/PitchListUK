PitchList UK Privacy Notice

How PitchList handles trial signups, billing access, support messages and vendor profile details.

Contact

Questions?

Email hello@pitchlist.uk with privacy or access questions.

Information We Collect

PitchList may collect details you submit through trial signup forms, support emails and vendor profile forms. This can include your name, business name, email, phone number, base postcode, trading radius, regions, business specialty and profile opt-in choices.

How We Use It

We use this information to provide pitch finder access, understand coverage needs, improve opportunity matching, handle support and maintain account records.

We also collect first-party site activity such as page views, pitch finder searches, checkout clicks, campaign tags and referrer hosts. This helps us understand whether a campaign is bringing people into the pitch finder. We do not store raw IP addresses in the activity monitor.

If you opt into a public vendor profile, selected business/profile details may later be shown to event organisers. Private billing information is not shown in public profiles.

Stripe Billing

Stripe handles card details, subscription billing, invoices and payment status. PitchList receives limited billing/access information such as email, customer ID, subscription ID and subscription status. PitchList does not store card numbers.

Email And Support

Emails sent to hello@pitchlist.uk and support messages may be stored so we can respond, track requests and avoid duplicate handling. We do not sell customer contact details.

Retention And Deletion

We keep operational records while they are useful for support, billing access, abuse prevention and service improvement. Email hello@pitchlist.uk if you want your submitted profile or request details corrected or removed.
