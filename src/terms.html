Subscription

7-day trial, then £4.99/month.

What PitchList Provides

PitchList UK provides a searchable list of public-source trader, stallholder, food vendor, market and event pitch opportunities. Rows may include source pages, application routes, locations, dates, categories, confidence markers and last-checked dates where available.

PitchList is a research and discovery tool. You should still check the source page before applying.

No Guaranteed Event Acceptance

No guaranteed event acceptance, pitch availability, sales, footfall, profitability, suitability or organiser response is promised. Organisers control their own applications, selection rules, deadlines and pricing.

Access And Fair Use

Your subscription is for your own business use. Do not resell, republish, scrape, bulk copy, or redistribute the database. Saved shortlist exports are for your own application planning.

Access may be limited or removed if the service is abused, attacked, copied at scale, or used in a way that harms PitchList, organisers, or other users.

Billing And Cancellation

The current UK database access price is £4.99 per month after the free trial. Stripe handles card collection, subscription billing, invoices and cancellation. Cancellation stops future renewals but does not automatically refund previous subscription periods.

If something has clearly gone wrong with access, email hello@pitchlist.uk and include the email used at checkout.

Data Freshness

PitchList records when rows were last checked and hides stale or unknown rows from customer mode where practical. Public pages can change, forms can close, and organisers can update details without notice.
