Why this exists

Most festival trader opportunities are not advertised in one place.

Organisers use different language: festival vendors wanted, festival traders wanted, food traders wanted, stallholders needed, trader applications open, concession applications, food truck pitches or catering pitches available. PitchList searches across those routes and turns the useful results into a searchable subscriber pitch finder.

What we check

Rows must have a practical next step.

  • Festival, event or organiser name
  • Location, region and trading area fit
  • Application form, trader pack or vendor information page
  • Published email/contact route where no form is available
  • Event date or date range where published
  • Food/vendor category fit where clear
  • Last checked date and confidence marker
  • No guarantee of acceptance — just a cleaner route to apply
